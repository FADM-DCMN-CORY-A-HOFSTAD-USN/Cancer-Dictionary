Clinical README: VirusTC Cancer Dictionary
==========================================

Overview
--------

The VirusTC Cancer Dictionary, also known as the Dictionary of Pycnogonid Infections and Treatments, provides clinical definitions and naturopathic treatment protocols for various oncological conditions. Developed in 2023 and early 2024, the dictionary attempts to explain cancer definitions based on clinical observations and data from the Fred Hutch Cancer Center.

Clinical Scope
--------------

This dictionary categorizes various types of cancers and disorders, including:

-   **Leukemias**: Covering acute lymphoblastic, acute myeloid, chronic lymphocytic, and chronic myeloid leukemias.

-   **Organ-Specific Cancers**: Including adrenal, bladder, breast, cervical, colon, kidney, liver, lung, ovarian, pancreatic, prostate, and stomach cancers.

-   **Specialized Conditions**: Addressing amyloidosis, aplastic anemia, autoimmune diseases, brain and spinal cord cancers, and cutaneous lymphoma.

-   **Pathological Framework**: The clinical approach focuses on the role of pycnogonid parasites and their impact on biological systems, including 3D mapping and tracking of viral loads through red and blue blood.
